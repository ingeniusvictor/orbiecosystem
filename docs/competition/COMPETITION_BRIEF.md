## Problem

Photovoltaic plants generate large volumes of operational data, alarms and maintenance records. Turning that information into timely, prioritized and explainable action remains difficult. When recoverable losses are not detected or prioritized, clean energy that could be delivered to the grid may remain unrealized.

## Proposed Solution

ORBI PBMetrics IA - Climate Recovery Edition is proposed as an operational intelligence layer for photovoltaic operations and maintenance. It is intended to help identify recoverable losses, explain the evidence behind recommendations, prioritize maintenance actions and verify recovered clean energy after intervention.

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

AI supports analysis, prioritization and explanation. It should surface hypotheses, evidence, confidence levels and limitations. It must not present hypotheses as definitive diagnoses.

## Human Supervision

The required human principle is: The AI recommends; the operator decides.

Technical teams retain final responsibility for operational decisions, maintenance execution and interpretation of field reality.

## Intended Renewable Efficiency Contribution

The intended contribution is to connect recoverable-loss identification with practical maintenance prioritization and post-action verification. Any real measurements, estimates, simulations or projections must be shown as distinct categories.
